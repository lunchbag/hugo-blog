Hey! Thanks for commenting. As promised, here's the full guide.

For the first five years of Lunch Money I was a company of one: no marketing team, no budget, and no one to hand any of this to. Every tactic in here is something I actually did, and none of them cost money. Where I've gone deeper on a method in a LinkedIn post or Instagram reel, the PDF links to it.

**What's inside:**

- **Get found** — showing up where your people already are, watching your competitors' discourse, evergreen content, podcasts and press
- **Give them a reason to choose you** — positioning against complaints, leading with your differentiator, branding and design
- **Be a person, not a company** — building in public, being the face of your company, talking to your first customers
- **Build things you own** — community, an email list, features that market themselves, amplifying user stories
- **Borrow other people's audiences** — founder friends at your stage, and partnerships you only pay for when they work
